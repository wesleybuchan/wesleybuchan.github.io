# PillTracker Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 30, 2026  
**Last updated:** August 30, 2026  
**Owner and Controller:** Wesley Buchan  
**App:** PillTracker

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PillTracker handles personal information. PillTracker is owned and operated by Wesley Buchan ("we," "us," or "our").

PillTracker is designed with a privacy-first approach. We do not sell personal information. Personally identifiable information and medication-related information entered into PillTracker remain on the customer’s device and are not transmitted to, stored on, or accessible by us, except where a customer independently chooses to use an optional feature that clearly states otherwise.

This policy is intended to address applicable privacy requirements in the United States and the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") for users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland, where applicable.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to the PillTracker mobile application, related support communications, and any associated services that link to this policy.

It does not apply to third-party websites, services, operating systems, app stores, device manufacturers, cloud-backup providers, notification providers, or other services that may process information under their own privacy notices.

## 3. Key Privacy Commitments

PillTracker is built around the following commitments:

- We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise monetize your personal information.
- We do not share your medication information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
- Personally identifiable information and medication-related data you enter in PillTracker remain on your device.
- We do not operate a server that receives, stores, analyzes, or gives us access to your medication records entered into the app.
- We do not use medication data to create advertising profiles or to make significant automated decisions about you.

## 4. Categories of Information

### Information stored locally in the app

Depending on how you use PillTracker, you may enter or generate information such as:

- Medication names, dosage instructions, schedules, refill dates, notes, and adherence history
- Reminder preferences and notification settings
- Optional profile details, such as a name or nickname, if the app provides such fields
- App preferences and locally generated app data

Medication-related information may be considered health information, sensitive personal information, or special-category personal data under certain privacy laws. PillTracker is designed so that this information remains locally on your device.

### Information you provide when contacting support

If you contact us, we may receive information you voluntarily provide, such as:

- Your name
- Your email address
- The content of your request or message
- Device type, operating-system version, app version, or technical details you choose to provide
- Any other information you voluntarily include in your communication

Please do not include unnecessary medical details in support messages. If you voluntarily provide health-related information in a support request, we will use it only as necessary to respond to that request, comply with law, protect our rights, or perform another purpose disclosed at the time of collection.

## 5. Sources and Purposes of Processing

We collect support-related information directly from you when you communicate with us. We use that information only to:

- Respond to questions, requests, bug reports, or support inquiries
- Troubleshoot technical issues and improve support operations
- Protect the security and integrity of the app and support systems
- Prevent fraud, abuse, or unlawful activity
- Maintain records, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable terms
- Comply with legal obligations

We do not use medication information entered in the app for these purposes because we do not receive that information from the app.

## 6. Disclosure and Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not disclose medication information stored in the app because it remains on your device and is not transmitted to us.

We may disclose limited support-related personal information only when necessary:

- To service providers that process information on our behalf, such as email, technical-support, app-distribution, or security providers
- To comply with applicable law, legal process, or a valid government request
- To protect the rights, property, safety, and security of PillTracker, our users, or others
- In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of all or part of our assets, subject to applicable legal requirements
- With your consent or at your direction

Where service providers process personal information on our behalf, we require them to use it only for authorized purposes and to maintain appropriate safeguards.

## 7. No Sale, No Sharing, and No Targeted Advertising

PillTracker does not sell personal information as defined by applicable U.S. state privacy laws. PillTracker also does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as defined by applicable U.S. state privacy laws.

PillTracker does not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you, does not process medication data for targeted advertising, and does not use medication data for marketing.

Because we do not sell or share personal information for these purposes, we do not respond to an opt-out request by changing those practices. If this changes, we will provide a clear opt-out mechanism and update this policy before beginning the new practice.

## 9. Health Information and HIPAA Notice

PillTracker is a consumer application and is not a healthcare provider, health plan, healthcare clearinghouse, or business associate acting on behalf of a HIPAA-covered entity, unless we expressly agree otherwise in writing. Accordingly, the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) generally does not govern information handled by PillTracker.

Even where HIPAA does not apply, we treat medication-related information as sensitive and have designed PillTracker so that it remains on your device. This policy does not create a HIPAA business-associate relationship, a provider-patient relationship, or any medical confidentiality obligation beyond what applicable law requires.

PillTracker is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, emergency services, pharmacy guidance, or medication instructions from a qualified healthcare professional.

## 10. Device Storage, Backups, and Permissions

### Local storage

Information you enter in PillTracker is stored locally on your device. You can generally access, correct, export if the app provides an export function, or delete the information through the app or your device settings.

### Backups and transfers

Your device may be configured to back up app data through Apple iCloud, Google services, a device manufacturer, a mobile carrier, or another third-party backup or transfer service. Depending on your device settings and the app’s technical configuration, locally stored app data may be included in those backups or device transfers.

Those third parties process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Review your device and backup settings if you do not want app data included in a backup or transferred to a new device.

### Notifications

If you enable reminders, PillTracker may use device-level notification permissions to display medication reminders. Notification content may be visible on a locked screen or other device surface depending on your device settings. You can manage notification permissions and visibility through your device settings.

## 11. Legal Bases Under GDPR

Where the GDPR applies, we process limited support-related personal data only when a valid legal basis exists, including:

- **Consent:** When you voluntarily provide information or enable an optional feature that requests consent
- **Performance of a contract:** When processing is necessary to provide requested support or app functionality
- **Legitimate interests:** When necessary to maintain app security, prevent abuse, troubleshoot issues, and improve support operations, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights
- **Legal obligation:** When processing is necessary to comply with applicable law

If you voluntarily submit health-related or other special-category data through a support request, we will process it only with your explicit consent or another lawful condition permitted under Article 9 of the GDPR.

## 12. GDPR Rights

If the GDPR applies to you, you may have the right to:

## 13. International Data Transfers

PillTracker does not intentionally transfer medication-related information from your device to us.

## 14. Retention and Security

### Retention

Medication-related information and other data entered into PillTracker remain on your device until you edit, delete, or uninstall the app, subject to device storage, backups, and operating-system behavior.

We retain support communications only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, protect our services, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations. We delete or anonymize support-related personal information when it is no longer needed, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

### Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect support-related information under our control. No system, device, storage method, or transmission method is completely secure.

Because PillTracker information is stored on your device, you should protect the device with a passcode, biometric authentication, device encryption where available, and current operating-system updates. Do not share your device credentials with others.
