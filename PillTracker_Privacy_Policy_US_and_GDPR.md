## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PillTracker handles personal information. PillTracker is owned and operated by Wesley Buchan ("we," "us," or "our").

PillTracker is designed with a privacy-first approach. We do not sell your medication information. Medication-related information and other details you enter into PillTracker remain on your device and are not transmitted to, stored on, or accessible by us.

Starting with version 1.5.0, PillTracker offers an optional Pro subscription and an optional one-time support purchase, and it shows advertising to users who have not purchased Pro. These features rely on three third-party services: Apple, RevenueCat, and Google AdMob. This policy explains what those services receive and the choices you have.

This policy is intended to address applicable privacy requirements in the United States and the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") for users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland, where applicable.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to the PillTracker mobile application, related support communications, and any associated services that link to this policy.

It does not apply to third-party websites, services, operating systems, app stores, device manufacturers, cloud-backup providers, notification providers, advertising providers, or other services that process information under their own privacy notices.

## 3. Key Privacy Commitments

- We do not sell your medication information, and we do not provide it to advertisers or advertising networks.
- Medication names, dosage instructions, schedules, notes, and adherence history you enter in PillTracker remain on your device.
- We do not operate a server that receives, stores, analyzes, or gives us access to your medication records entered into the app.
- Advertising in PillTracker is not based on your medication data. The ad service receives device and ad-request information, not the contents of your medication records.
- We do not use medication data to create advertising profiles or to make significant automated decisions about you.
- Pro subscribers do not see ads.

## 4. Categories of Information

### Information stored locally in the app

Depending on how you use PillTracker, you may enter or generate information such as:

- Medication names, dosage instructions, schedules, refill dates, notes, and adherence history
- Reminder preferences and notification settings
- Optional profile details, such as a name or nickname, if the app provides such fields
- App preferences, app lock settings, and locally generated app data

Medication-related information may be considered health information, sensitive personal information, or special-category personal data under certain privacy laws. PillTracker is designed so that this information remains locally on your device.

### Information you provide when contacting support

If you contact us, we may receive information you voluntarily provide, such as:

- Your name and email address
- The content of your request or message
- Device type, operating-system version, app version, or technical details you choose to provide
- Any other information you voluntarily include in your communication

Please do not include unnecessary medical details in support messages. If you voluntarily provide health-related information in a support request, we will use it only as necessary to respond to that request, comply with law, protect our rights, or perform another purpose disclosed at the time of collection.

### Purchase and subscription information

When you view the Pro offer, subscribe, make a support purchase, or restore purchases, the following information is processed:

- An anonymous app user identifier generated by RevenueCat (we do not require you to create an account)
- Product identifiers, purchase and renewal dates, subscription status, and entitlement status (for example, whether Pro is active)
- Transaction identifiers and receipt data from the Apple App Store
- Device and app details such as app version, operating-system version, device model, country or region, and IP address

We do not receive your name, Apple ID, credit card number, or other payment details. Payments are processed by Apple.

### Advertising and device information

For users who have not purchased Pro, the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) may collect or receive information such as:

- IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of a device
- Device identifiers, including the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking, and other device or app identifiers
- Device and software details, such as device model, operating-system version, language, and app information
- Ad interaction and performance data, such as ad requests, impressions, and clicks
- Diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information related to the ads SDK
- Your consent choices, where a consent message is shown

AdMob does not receive the contents of your medication records.

## 7. Subscriptions and Purchases

PillTracker offers the following optional purchases, which are shown with their price in the app before you confirm:

- **PillTracker Pro:** an auto-renewing monthly subscription that removes ads and supports the development of the app.
- **Support purchase:** a one-time purchase that supports the development of the app.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your device's Settings under your Apple ID, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Refund requests are handled by Apple.

You can restore previous purchases from within the app. Apple's standard license terms for apps, including the [Apple Standard End User License Agreement](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/), apply to your use of the app and purchases unless we provide separate terms.

## 8. Advertising, Tracking, and Your Choices

PillTracker shows banner advertising provided by Google AdMob to users who have not purchased Pro. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized, depending on your device settings, your location, and your consent choices.

Your choices include:

- **Purchase Pro:** Pro subscribers do not see ads.
- **App Tracking Transparency (iOS):** On iPhone and iPad, the app may ask permission to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you decline, or if you turn tracking off in Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking, the iOS advertising identifier is not made available to the app and ads are less personalized.
- **Consent message:** In the EEA, the United Kingdom, and other regions where required, a consent message may appear when you first use the app. You can accept or decline, and you can change your choice where the app or Google provides a privacy options control.
- **Google ad personalization:** You can manage ad personalization for your Google account at [Google My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/).
- **Contact us:** You can contact us using the information in Section 19 to ask about ad-related data or to make a privacy request.

Ads are provided by Google and are not selected by us based on any medication information. Clicking an ad may take you to a third-party website or app that is governed by its own privacy policy.

## 10. Sale, Sharing, and Targeted Advertising (U.S.)

We do not sell personal information for money, and we do not sell or share your medication information.

Some U.S. state privacy laws define "sale," "sharing," or "targeted advertising" broadly to include making device identifiers and ad-interaction data available to an advertising provider so that ads can be delivered or personalized. When personalized ads are shown through AdMob, the disclosure of device identifiers and ad-interaction data to Google may be considered a sale, sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising, or targeted advertising under those laws.

To the extent these laws apply, you may opt out by:

- Turning off tracking for PillTracker in your iPhone's Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking, or declining the tracking request in the app
- Purchasing Pro, which removes ads
- Contacting us at the email address in Section 19 with the subject line "Do Not Sell or Share"

We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information, including medication information, for advertising. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16 years of age.
